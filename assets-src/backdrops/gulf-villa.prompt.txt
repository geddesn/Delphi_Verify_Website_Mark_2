Photorealistic medium-format architectural photograph: a contemporary luxury
villa in Abu Dhabi at dusk, lit from within, with no people anywhere in the
frame.

SCENE
A modern private villa on a quiet residential plot in Abu Dhabi, photographed
from the garden at blue hour. Confident contemporary Gulf architecture: crisp
horizontal slabs and deep cantilevered overhangs in pale limestone and
fair-faced concrete, tall full-height glazing set back into shade, a screened
terrace, a low boundary wall. A still reflecting pool in the foreground holding
the building's lights. Mature date palms and low desert planting - gravel,
agave, olive - around the terrace. The sky has gone deep indigo with the last
warm light low on the horizon.

The villa is warmly lit from inside: glowing interiors behind the glazing, low
concealed lighting under the overhangs washing the stone. Calm, empty and
immaculately kept.

This is a permanent private home of serious architectural quality - not a
resort, not a hotel, not a holiday villa.

NO PEOPLE
There are no people anywhere in this image. No residents, no staff, no figures
on the terrace, at the glazing, or inside the villa. No silhouettes.

STRICTLY NOT
No gold, no gilding, no ornate arches, no domes, no minarets, no carved
screens, no marble excess, no chandeliers, no fountains with statuary. No
supercars, no helicopter. No infinity pool spilling toward a horizon, no
sun loungers, no parasols, no towels, no cocktails, no fire pit, no torches.
No skyline of glass towers, no Dubai-style spectacle. No coloured or party
lighting, no neon, no uplit palms in purple or blue. Not a rendering, not a
CGI look, not an estate agent's photograph. Not Mediterranean, not Californian.

COMPOSITION - IMPORTANT
16:9 landscape. This image will be used as a website background, anchored to
the RIGHT side of the page and faded out toward the left. Therefore: place the
villa and all the visual weight in the RIGHT two thirds of the frame, running
away from the viewer. Keep the LEFT third quiet and simple - still dark water,
empty terrace and indigo sky only, nothing detailed or important there. Low
natural eye-level perspective from the garden. Verticals corrected and true.

The subject must read instantly, at a glance, as a modern luxury villa - a
clear, legible silhouette of slabs, overhangs and lit glazing against the dusk,
not a confusing tangle of detail.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Deep indigo dusk with the villa clearly the brightest thing in the frame -
warm interior and concealed light on pale stone against a cool dark sky and
garden. Strong, clean tonal separation between the lit building and the dark
surroundings. Rich blacks that stay black. Neutral filmic grade, no
orange-and-teal, no HDR glow, no heavy bloom, no lens flare.

STYLE
Premium commercial architectural photography for a brand campaign - calm,
still and understated, the visual language of an architecture title. Long
exposure stillness in the water. High resolution, believable reflections,
accurate architectural proportion.

AVOID
Text, lettering, numbers or logos of any kind anywhere, including house numbers
and signage. People of any kind. Vehicles. Holograms, glowing overlays,
futuristic effects, data visualisations. Visible third-party brands.
Watermarks. Distorted or impossible geometry.
