Photorealistic medium-format photograph: a business jet standing in a lit
hangar at dusk, with no people anywhere in the frame.

SCENE
A mid-size business jet parked inside a clean private aviation hangar at the
end of the day. The hangar doors stand open to a dark apron and a deep indigo
sky beyond. Inside, the hangar lighting is on: the polished white fuselage
catches it along its length, the wing and engine nacelle picking up clean
highlights, bare metal leading edges glinting. Pale sealed concrete floor
holding a soft reflection of the aircraft. A wheeled maintenance stand and
neatly coiled ground cables to one side. Everything ordered, swept and quiet.

Restrained and expensive. A valuable asset at rest, kept to a standard.

NO PEOPLE
There are no people anywhere in this image. No crew, no engineers, no ground
staff, no figures on the apron or in the hangar. No silhouettes.

STRICTLY NOT
No gold, no VIP cabin interior, no stairs with a red carpet, no champagne, no
models. Not a passenger terminal, not an airliner, not an air show, not a
museum. Not a dirty or chaotic workshop. No coloured or party lighting, no
neon. No skyline of glass towers, no Dubai-style spectacle. Not a rendering,
not a CGI look.

COMPOSITION - IMPORTANT
16:9 landscape. This image will be used as a website background, anchored to
the RIGHT side of the page and faded out toward the left. Therefore: place the
aircraft and all the visual weight in the RIGHT two thirds of the frame, nose
toward the left, running away from the viewer. Keep the LEFT third quiet and
simple - dark hangar interior and empty floor only, nothing detailed or
important there. Natural eye-level perspective from inside the hangar.
Verticals corrected and true.

The subject must read instantly, at a glance, as a private jet - a clear,
legible silhouette of fuselage, wing, tail and engine, not a confusing tangle
of detail.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Dark hangar and indigo dusk beyond, with the aircraft clearly the brightest
thing in the frame - warm white artificial light on the fuselage against cool
dark surroundings. Strong, clean tonal separation between the lit aircraft and
the dark hangar. Rich blacks that stay black. Neutral filmic grade, no
orange-and-teal, no HDR glow, no heavy bloom, no lens flare, no light streaks.

STYLE
Premium commercial photography for a brand campaign - calm, still and precise,
the visual language of an aviation or engineering title. High resolution,
believable reflections in the fuselage and on the floor, accurate aeronautical
proportion.

AVOID
Text, lettering, numbers or logos of any kind anywhere, including registration
marks, tail numbers, manufacturer badges, warning placards, floor markings and
hangar signage. People of any kind. Holograms, glowing overlays, futuristic
effects, data visualisations. Visible third-party brands. Watermarks.
Distorted or impossible geometry.
