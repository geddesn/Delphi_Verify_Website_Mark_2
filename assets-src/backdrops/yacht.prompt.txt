Photorealistic medium-format photograph: a large motor yacht lying at her berth
at night on the Cote d'Azur, lit up, with no people anywhere in the frame.

SCENE
A superyacht of perhaps fifty metres, berthed stern-to on the quay of a
Mediterranean harbour on the French Riviera - Antibes, Saint-Tropez or Monaco
in character. Blue hour has passed and the sky is deep indigo, almost black.
The vessel is fully lit: warm deck lighting under the overhangs, floodlit mast
and superstructure, glowing saloon windows, underwater lights turning the water
around the transom a soft green. Oiled teak decks catch the light. Stainless
rails, white composite superstructure, a neatly stowed tender. Beyond her, the
soft out-of-focus lights of the old harbour and the hillside town rising behind.
Water almost flat, holding long reflections of the vessel's lights.

Restrained and expensive. A beautiful, well-kept vessel at rest - not a party,
not an advertisement.

NO PEOPLE
There are no people anywhere in this image. No crew, no guests, no figures on
deck, on the quay, or in the windows. No silhouettes.

STRICTLY NOT
No champagne, no glassware, no table settings, no music, no fireworks, no
coloured party lighting, no neon, no pink or purple uplighting. No helicopter,
no jet ski, no supercar on the quay. Not a rendering, not a CGI look. Not a
charter brochure. No drone or high aerial view - this is seen from the quay at
human height. Not Caribbean, not Gulf, not Miami.

COMPOSITION - IMPORTANT
16:9 landscape. This image will be used as a website background, anchored to
the RIGHT side of the page and faded out toward the left. Therefore: place the
yacht and all the visual weight in the RIGHT two thirds of the frame, running
away from the viewer. Keep the LEFT third quiet and simple - open dark water
and a few distant harbour lights only, nothing detailed or important there.
Natural eye-level perspective from the quayside. Verticals corrected and true.

The subject must read instantly, at a glance, as a yacht - a clear, legible
silhouette of hull, superstructure and mast against the night, not a confusing
tangle of detail.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Deep indigo and near-black night, with the vessel clearly the brightest thing
in the frame - warm white deck and interior light against cool dark water and
sky. Strong, clean tonal separation between the lit vessel and the dark
surroundings. Rich blacks that stay black. Neutral filmic grade, no
orange-and-teal, no HDR glow, no heavy bloom, no lens flare, no light streaks.

STYLE
Premium commercial marine photography for a brand campaign - calm, still and
understated. Long exposure stillness in the water. High resolution, believable
reflections, accurate naval architecture and proportion.

AVOID
Text, lettering, numbers or logos of any kind anywhere, including vessel names,
hailing ports, registration marks, flags and equipment badges. People of any
kind. Holograms, glowing overlays, futuristic effects, data visualisations.
Visible third-party brands. Watermarks. Distorted or impossible geometry.
