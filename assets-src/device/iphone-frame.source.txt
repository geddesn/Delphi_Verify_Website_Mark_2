WHAT IS TRANSPARENT
The screen as well as the background — alpha 0 in both, 77.7% of the image.
Only the phone body is opaque (21.3%; the remaining 1.0% is edge
antialiasing). That is what makes the frame useful: a rendering sits BEHIND
the image and shows through the screen, and the opaque bezel paints over it.

No CSS rounding or notch mask is therefore needed. The bezel's own rounded
corners and the notch cover the rectangle behind them, exactly as a physical
device does. Give the screen a plain rectangle and the frame does the masking.

THE SCREEN APERTURE — MEASURED, NOT EYEBALLED
Flood-filled the transparent region inwards from the border; whatever stayed
transparent and unreached is the screen hole.

  bounding box   x 77-850, y 72-1751
  size           774 x 1680  (97.6% of the bbox — the shortfall is the notch)
  ratio          0.4607, against the iPhone's own 390/844 = 0.4621

  left    8.297%     top     3.947%
  width  83.405%     height 92.105%

Those four percentages are in `phoneScreen` in src/content/renderings.ts.

⚠️  REPLACE THIS IMAGE AND THEY MUST BE MEASURED AGAIN. A different frame puts
the screen somewhere else, and the renderings will sit crooked inside it with
nothing failing loudly enough to notice.
