ONE BUILDING
An ordinary new-build apartment in Colombia, inland tropical city, mid-rise

THE SIX DEFECTS

1 TOP-LEFT. LEAKING JOINT. A white PVC pipe joint on bare blockwork with a wet

2 TOP-CENTRE. CRACKED TILE. A finished pale ceramic floor with one tile
clearly cracked across its face and the grout around it crumbling and uneven,

angle.

3 TOP-RIGHT. UNSAFE WIRING. An electrical outlet box on a block wall with no
cover plate, bare conductor ends twisted together and hanging loose out of the

4 BOTTOM-LEFT. DAMP. A block wall with a broad irregular damp patch darkening

5 BOTTOM-CENTRE. BAD PLASTER. A white painted wall that is visibly uneven —

6 BOTTOM-RIGHT. MISALIGNED DOOR. A plain white internal door hung badly in its
