Photorealistic construction and interior photography presented as a 3x2 GRID OF
SIX SEPARATE PHOTOGRAPHS in one image, nobody present in any frame.

THE GRID
Three columns, two rows. Each cell is a complete photograph filling its cell to
all four edges. Cells butt edge to edge. No border, gutter, white strip,
dividing line, frame, rounded corners, drop shadow or numbering.

ONE BUILDING
An ordinary new-build apartment in Colombia, inland tropical city, mid-rise
residential tower. Grey concrete block and poured slabs where unfinished; white
paint, pale ceramic tile and plain white joinery where finished. Economical,
NOT luxury: no marble, no stone, no timber panelling, no styling, no furniture.
Flat working daylight.

THE SIX

1 TOP-LEFT. KITCHEN PLUMBING, ANOTHER WALL. Bare blockwork with white PVC waste
pipe rising from the slab and two capped water points chased in beside it,
lower down the wall than usual, with the floor and skirting line visible.
Square-on.

2 TOP-CENTRE. BATHROOM PLUMBING, FROM THE CORNER. A small bathroom in bare
block seen from a corner rather than the doorway, showing two walls at once:
the sanitary stub in the floor on one, supply points on the other, grey
waterproofing turned up both.

3 TOP-RIGHT. LIVING ROOM ELECTRICAL, CEILING. Looking up at a concrete soffit
with orange conduit running across it to a ceiling rose box, coiled wire
hanging from the box, the tops of two block walls meeting at the corner of
frame.

4 BOTTOM-LEFT. KITCHEN FINISHED, FROM THE DOORWAY. The same kind of small
fitted kitchen seen from further back through the doorway: one continuous run
of plain white cabinets, light worktop, stainless sink, white tiled
splashback, pale tiled floor in the foreground. Empty.

5 BOTTOM-CENTRE. BATHROOM FINISHED, FROM INSIDE. A small finished bathroom
photographed from inside beside the shower rather than from the door: pale
large-format tiles, white WC and basin on the far wall, a mirror above the
basin. Empty, nothing on any surface.

6 BOTTOM-RIGHT. BEDROOM FINISHED, WINDOW WALL. An empty bedroom shot toward
the aluminium window with white walls either side, pale tiled floor, the edge
of a built-in white wardrobe just entering the left of frame.

HOW THEY ARE PHOTOGRAPHED
Record photographs, not architecture. Square-on where stated, verticals true,
even light, everything legible including corners and floor. Taken on a phone by
somebody documenting their own work. No shafts of sun, no vignette, no shallow
depth of field, no staging.

STRICTLY NOT
No people anywhere, including reflections. No furniture, decoration, plants or
towels. No labels, captions, arrows, numbering or watermarks. NO WRITING
ANYWHERE IN ANY FRAME.

CRITICAL - SIX PLAIN PHOTOGRAPHS
All six are straight photographs. Nothing superimposed: no panels, cards,
boxes, callouts, annotations, user interface, diagrams, markers, arrows or
lines over any picture.

COMPOSITION
Each cell is 16:9 landscape. Eye level except cell 3, which looks up.
Verticals true, no fisheye, no wide-angle stretch at the edges.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Flat neutral daylight in every frame, consistent across all six and matching a
grey dusty shell in cells 1-3 and a clean white finish in cells 4-6. Neutral
grade, no saturation push, no HDR.

AVOID
Lettering, numbers or logos of any kind. Distorted geometry. Any visible seam
between cells. Anything that looks expensive or styled.
