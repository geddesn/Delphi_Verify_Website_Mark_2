Photorealistic studio photography presented as a 3x2 GRID OF SIX SEPARATE
PHOTOGRAPHS in one image: six sculptural objects, each photographed alone on
the same set, under the same light.

THE GRID
Three columns, two rows. Each cell is a complete photograph filling its cell
to all four edges. Cells butt edge to edge. No border, no gutter, no white
strip, no dividing line, no frame, no rounded corners, no drop shadow, no
numbering, no caption.

ONE SET, ONE LIGHT
Every frame is shot on the same seamless deep navy-black surface — very dark,
faintly reflective, like polished slate. One cool key light rakes in from the
upper left, and one narrow indigo-blue accent light picks out an edge. Deep
shadow, controlled highlights, shallow depth of field, macro lens, museum
lighting. Materials are real and expensive: black glass, brushed steel,
machined aluminium, optical crystal. Cold, precise, still.

NO TEXT ANYWHERE. No lettering, no numerals, no dials, no markings, no
labels, no logos, no watermarks. No screens showing an interface. No hands,
no people, no brand marks. Nothing that looks like a user interface or a
diagram.

THE SIX

1 TOP-LEFT — DEVICE. A single slab of black glass with polished bevelled
edges, standing upright on the surface like a monolith, catching one long
cool highlight down its face. Nothing on it. Its reflection runs into the
dark below it.

2 TOP-CENTRE — APPLICATION. Four rectangular steel frames of decreasing size,
set one behind another and perfectly concentric, forming a tunnel that
recedes into the dark. The innermost opening glows a soft indigo. A single
controlled way in.

3 TOP-RIGHT — CAPTURE. A precision iris of overlapping machined steel blades,
seen straight on, open about a third. Cool light spills through the opening.
Every blade edge is crisp; the machining is visible.

4 BOTTOM-LEFT — TIME. Twelve identical thin steel plates stacked horizontally
with even gaps between them, receding slightly from the camera. One plate,
about a third of the way down, is lit from within so its edge glows indigo
while the others stay dark. One moment among many, marked.

5 BOTTOM-CENTRE — LOCATION. A dark plane ruled with a fine grid, running away
to a low horizon. A single narrow vertical beam of indigo light lands on one
intersection of the grid and pools slightly where it meets the surface.
Nothing else on the plane.

6 BOTTOM-RIGHT — MEDIA INTEGRITY. Two identical faceted optical-crystal
prisms standing side by side, exactly matched in size, angle and cut, one a
little behind the other. Cool light passes through both and lands identically.
They are unmistakably the same object twice.

STRICTLY AVOID
Text of any kind. Interfaces, dashboards, app screens, charts, icons, maps
with labels. People, hands, faces. Warm light, gold, brass, wood, plants,
fabric. Clutter, props, tools, background objects. Lens flare, heavy bloom,
neon, cyberpunk. Anything that reads as stock 3D or as a technology
illustration rather than as a photograph of a real object.
