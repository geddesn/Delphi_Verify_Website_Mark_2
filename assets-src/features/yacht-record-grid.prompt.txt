WHY ONE GENERATION RATHER THAN NINE
Nine independent generations produce nine different yachts. The moment they sit
together in a record, mismatched leather and joinery read as a stock library
rather than one vessel's condition on one day — which is the whole point of the
set. One generation is what holds the design language identical across all
nine. It also costs 9 credits instead of 81.

WHY THE SALOON IS IN HERE TWICE
Frames 1 and 2 are the same saloon, intact and torn. This supersedes the
separate saloon-before-after take, whose setting read as mid-market: the low
table was plain, the joinery flat, and nothing in the room said superyacht.
The v1 brief asked for exactly that — "restrained", "plain", "expensive without
ornament", "no artwork" — and got it. This brief inverts those instructions.
