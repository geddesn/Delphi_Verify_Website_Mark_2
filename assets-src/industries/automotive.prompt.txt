Photorealistic medium-format photograph: a fleet handover agent documenting the
condition of a prestige vehicle at handover in Abu Dhabi, with a smartphone.

SCENE
A single dark grey executive saloon standing on smooth pale concrete beneath a
deep architectural canopy - a private residence forecourt or the covered
delivery bay of a discreet prestige dealership in Abu Dhabi. Hard Gulf daylight
outside the canopy, cool shade within, the car's flank catching a long band of
reflected light. Clean, empty, ordered surroundings: fair-faced concrete, a
line of palms and low desert planting beyond, nothing cluttering the frame.

The agent is examining the front wing and door - the panel a damage dispute
would actually turn on.

STRICTLY NOT
No supercar, no sports car, no bright paint, no chrome excess, no gold, no
spoilers, no alloy close-ups. Not a showroom full of cars, not a car park, not
a racetrack, not a desert dune shoot. No skyline of glass towers, no Dubai-style
spectacle. Not a rental counter, not a garage workshop, not a scrapyard.

SUBJECT
One handover agent in understated smart clothing - a plain muted shirt, no tie,
no name badge, no visible branding.

Photographed OVER THE SHOULDER, from behind and slightly beside, so the camera
shares their viewpoint and looks where they are looking. The head is seen in
profile or near profile with eyes on the panel they are documenting. That is
correct and intended. The subject must never look at, turn toward, or make eye
contact with the camera, and must not be aware of it.

ANATOMY - CRITICAL
Exactly two arms and exactly two hands. Both hands are on the phone and nowhere
else. No third arm, no spare hand, no floating limb. Five fingers per hand,
correctly jointed.

THE PHONE
Held in both hands, low and toward the lower left of the frame, occupying only
a small part of the picture. The screen shows the live camera view of the same
body panel, correctly aligned with what is really there, as a photograph being
composed. The screen is a clean camera image only: no app interface, no
buttons, no icons, no symbols, no numbers, no words, no shutter button.

COMPOSITION
16:9 landscape. The agent occupies the right third; the vehicle runs away to
the left, seen along its flank rather than head on. Verticals corrected and
true. One obvious subject, uncluttered, so the picture reads clearly when
displayed small. Natural eye-level perspective. Agent and phone sharp, the far
end of the car gently soft.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Warm neutral palette - pale concrete, sand, graphite paintwork, muted stone.
Strong directional Gulf daylight outside, controlled shade within, real shadow
structure, highlights held rather than blown. Neutral filmic grade. No
saturation push, no orange-and-teal, no HDR flatness.

STYLE
Premium commercial photography for a brand campaign - restrained automotive
photography in an architectural setting, calm and confident. High resolution,
realistic skin texture, accurate smartphone geometry, believable reflections in
paintwork and glass.

AVOID
Text, lettering, numbers or logos of any kind anywhere, including number
plates, badges, marque emblems, wheel centres and signage. Number plates must
be blank. The subject facing, turning toward or making eye contact with the
camera. A third arm or extra limb. Holograms, floating interface panels,
glowing overlays. Blockchain, padlock or shield symbols. Data visualisations.
Generic stock-photo poses, handshakes, thumbs-up, key handovers. Distorted or
extra fingers, warped phone. Watermarks.
