Photorealistic medium-format architectural photograph: a site manager
documenting build state inside a high-specification villa under construction in
Abu Dhabi, with a smartphone.

SCENE
The interior of a contemporary low-rise villa on a well-run Abu Dhabi
development, at first-fix stage. Reinforced concrete frame, fair-faced
blockwork, clean structural openings of generous height framing hard Gulf
daylight. Services exposed and about to be covered: conduit runs and back
boxes, copper and insulated pipework, ducting, a distribution board enclosure,
insulation between framing. A stack of plain plasterboard resting flat against
a wall. Fine construction dust in the light.

The site is exceptionally tidy - materials stacked square, floor swept, no
debris. A premium development run to a high standard, not a rough site.

STRICTLY NOT
No gold, no mirrored or gold-tinted glass, no supercars, no skyline of glass
towers, no Dubai-style spectacle. Nothing flashy. Not a finished interior, not
a showhome, not a render. Not a muddy or chaotic site. Not European or American
construction - Gulf blockwork and concrete frame. No BIM or CAD overlays.

SUBJECT
One site manager in clean, correct site PPE: plain hi-vis vest in a muted,
slightly desaturated safety yellow, plain hard hat, long sleeves, work boots.
Every item completely unmarked - no printing, no logo, no lettering, no
stickers, no name tape.

Photographed OVER THE SHOULDER, from behind and slightly beside him, so the
camera shares his viewpoint and looks where he is looking. His head is seen in
profile or near profile with his eyes down on the work. That is correct and
intended. He must never look at, turn toward, or make eye contact with the
camera, and must not be aware of it.

ANATOMY - CRITICAL
He has exactly two arms and exactly two hands. Both hands are on the phone and
nowhere else. There is no third arm, no spare hand, no floating limb, no second
right arm reaching elsewhere in the frame. Five fingers per hand, correctly
jointed.

THE PHONE
Held in both hands, low and toward the lower left of the frame, occupying only
a small part of the picture. The screen shows the live camera view of this same
work area, correctly aligned with what is really in front of it, as a
photograph being composed. The screen is a clean camera image only: no app
interface, no buttons, no icons, no symbols, no numbers, no words, no shutter
button.

COMPOSITION
16:9 landscape. The manager occupies the right third; the exposed services and
structure open away to the left. Verticals corrected and true. One obvious
subject, uncluttered, so the picture reads clearly when displayed small.
Natural eye-level perspective. Subject and phone sharp, the far side of the
space gently soft.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Warm neutral palette - concrete grey, sand, buff block, pale timber. Strong
directional Gulf daylight through the structural openings, controlled and
shaped, with real shadow structure and highlights held rather than blown.
Neutral filmic grade. Calm and premium, not gritty photojournalism. No
saturation push, no orange-and-teal, no HDR flatness.

STYLE
Premium commercial architectural photography for a brand campaign - the visual
language of an architecture title documenting a building under construction.
High resolution, realistic skin texture, accurate smartphone geometry.

AVOID
Text, lettering, numbers or logos of any kind anywhere, including on PPE,
signage, packaging, plasterboard, pipework and scaffolding. A third arm or any
extra limb. The subject facing, turning toward or making eye contact with the
camera. Holograms, floating interface panels, glowing overlays. Blockchain,
padlock or shield symbols. Cyber-security graphics, data visualisations.
Generic stock-photo poses, handshakes, thumbs-up, hard-hat-and-clipboard
cliches. Visible third-party brands. Distorted or extra fingers, warped phone.
Watermarks.
