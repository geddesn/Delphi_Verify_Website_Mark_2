Photorealistic medium-format photograph: a yacht captain documenting the
condition of a motor yacht at redelivery in a Marbella marina, with a
smartphone.

SCENE
The aft deck of a well-kept motor yacht of perhaps forty metres, berthed stern
to the quay in a Mediterranean marina near Marbella. Oiled teak decking laid in
neat seams, stainless rails and cleats, white composite superstructure, a
folded tender cover, coiled mooring lines. Beyond the transom, the quay, other
masts and the dry Andalusian hills in soft haze. Early morning, before the day
gets busy - the vessel is being checked over, not shown off.

STRICTLY NOT
No champagne, no glassware, no guests, no swimwear, no models, no party. No
gold fittings, no leopard print, no supercar on the quay, no helicopter. Not a
brochure or charter advertisement. No drone or aerial view. No sunset, no
lens flare. Not Caribbean, not Gulf - this is the Mediterranean coast of Spain.

SUBJECT
One captain or first officer in plain, well-cut crew clothing - a muted polo or
shirt in navy or stone, no epaulettes, no name badge, no visible branding.

Photographed OVER THE SHOULDER, from behind and slightly beside, so the camera
shares their viewpoint and looks where they are looking. The head is seen in
profile or near profile with eyes on the deck they are documenting. That is
correct and intended. The subject must never look at, turn toward, or make eye
contact with the camera, and must not be aware of it.

ANATOMY - CRITICAL
Exactly two arms and exactly two hands. Both hands are on the phone and nowhere
else. No third arm, no spare hand, no floating limb. Five fingers per hand,
correctly jointed.

THE PHONE
Held in both hands, low and toward the lower left of the frame, occupying only
a small part of the picture. The screen shows the live camera view of the same
stretch of deck and transom, correctly aligned with what is really there, as a
photograph being composed. The screen is a clean camera image only: no app
interface, no buttons, no icons, no symbols, no numbers, no words, no shutter
button.

COMPOSITION
16:9 landscape. The officer occupies the right third; the deck and the marina
open away to the left. Verticals corrected and true. One obvious subject,
uncluttered, so the picture reads clearly when displayed small. Natural
eye-level perspective. Subject and phone sharp, the marina beyond gently soft.

LIGHT AND COLOUR
Warm neutral palette - teak, bone white, stainless grey, muted navy. Soft
directional early daylight with real shadow structure. The sea and sky
restrained and slightly desaturated, never postcard blue. Neutral filmic grade.
No saturation push, no orange-and-teal, no HDR flatness.

STYLE
Premium commercial photography for a brand campaign - the visual language of a
serious marine title, calm and understated. High resolution, realistic skin
texture, accurate smartphone geometry, believable reflections.

AVOID
Text, lettering, numbers or logos of any kind anywhere, including vessel names,
hailing ports, registration marks and equipment badges. The subject facing,
turning toward or making eye contact with the camera. A third arm or extra
limb. Holograms, floating interface panels, glowing overlays. Blockchain,
padlock or shield symbols. Data visualisations. Generic stock-photo poses,
handshakes, thumbs-up. Visible third-party brands. Distorted or extra fingers,
warped phone. Watermarks.
