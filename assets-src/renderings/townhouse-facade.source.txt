ALPHA REMOVED ON THE WAY IN
The source carries an alpha channel that is 0.00% non-opaque — dead weight in
every derived file. Stripped here rather than in the pipeline so the master
matches what ships.

THE RESOLUTION CEILING — READ BEFORE CHANGING THE LAYOUT
736x772 is small, and it decides how the capture preview can be composed.

  full-bleed phone (390/844)  crops to 357x772   too narrow
  4:3 portrait                crops to 579x772   too narrow
  near-square preview         718x772            fine

So the preview CANNOT go full-bleed portrait behind the whole screen; that
crop leaves 357px of width for a window needing ~590 physical px, and it would
visibly soften. A contained preview is also what the real app does — see
screenshots_capture.png, where the preview sits between the certificate pill
and the shutter — so this is consistent rather than a compromise.

PANNING
There is room. A preview of roughly 358x420 canvas px renders at ~295 CSS px
wide, or ~590 physical at 2x, leaving about 146px horizontal and 80px vertical
of the master unused — a 20-25% range to drift within without upscaling.
Enough for a slow live-camera pan; not enough for a sweep.
