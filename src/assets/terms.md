## 2. What verification means

A Delphi Verify certificate reports the checks performed on specified media and whether the media and published metadata presented for verification match the values committed when the evidence record was sealed. Depending on the certificate, those checks may include controlled in-app capture, device or application attestation, capture-time and location signals, reproduction screening, automated fraud analysis and cryptographic integrity checks.

These checks are strong technical signals, not absolute guarantees. A certificate does not by itself establish a person's identity or authority; ownership, value, condition or legal title; the accuracy of user-written titles or descriptions; that a scene was not arranged before capture; that everything relevant was captured; what happened before or after capture; or that an asset remains in the condition shown. Automated and AI-assisted assessments are probabilistic and may produce false positives, false negatives or incomplete results.
